Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about stocking, ordering, and product availability at Silverstar Roofing Supplies.

Do you sell wholesale only?

Yes. Silverstar Roofing Supplies is a wholesale roofing materials supplier. We do not provide installation services.

What products do you carry?

We stock residential shingles, commercial roofing systems, and roofing accessories including tools, underlayment, metal accessories, vents, and downspouts.

Can I get a quote by phone?

Absolutely. Call 647 537 4486 and our team can help with pricing and product availability.

Where are you located?

We are located at 160 Silver Star Blvd, Scarborough, ON M1V 5P2. You can also view our hours and directions on the Location & Hours page.

Do you carry multiple roofing brands?

Yes. We carry roofing materials from leading brands across residential and commercial categories. See our Partners page for more information.
